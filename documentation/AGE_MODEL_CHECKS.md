# Age-model adequacy checks

Primary quadratic models were adopted during the post-audit reanalysis after linear-model predictive checks revealed residual curvature. To limit additional parameters, the child-only model shares one quadratic coefficient across languages, and the joint/format models share one coefficient per source across languages (and across formats in the format model). This is a data-informed revision, not a preregistered universal developmental prediction.

| Family | Linear-only squared-age discrepancy probability | Primary quadratic discrepancy probability |
|---|---:|---:|
| Child rate | 0.9243 | 0.4725 |
| Joint rate | 0.9930 | 0.6314 |
| Repair formats | 0.9945 | 0.5886 |

These probabilities compare draw-paired replicated and observed discrepancies. The extreme linear-only joint/format values identify an age-pattern mismatch; the corresponding primary checks do not show that mismatch. They are not conventional p-values or out-of-sample model rankings. Other features are checked separately in the full diagnostic tables.

Linear-only models are actual refits retained in models/sensitivity. Endpoint contrasts should not be used to deny nonlinear variation between their two endpoints.
